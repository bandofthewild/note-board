band drill · rough build

Same pitch down each column, transposed and spelled per instrument. Pick a set, hit play, and the board pulses through the notes in time.

Using Your Own Music

Loading A Track

  • Click Load a Song and pick an audio file from this computer. Nothing is uploaded — it is analyzed right here.
  • The app finds the tempo and downbeat, then plays the track while the board rides the same clock, so they cannot drift apart.
  • Works best on a clean recording with a clear beat. Soft orchestral or MIDI tracks are harder to read.

If It Reads Wrong

  • Tempo at half or double speed? Use ÷ or ×. For a waltz set Beats/bar to 3 first, which switches those to ÷3 / ×3.
  • Board on the wrong beat of the bar? Press Play and tap Beat 1 (or the spacebar) right on the downbeat — it comes in one full bar later, tempo untouched.
  • Slightly ahead or behind? Nudge with ±20ms.
  • Save Song remembers all of it, including later tweaks.

Music From Elsewhere

  • Playing from a phone, a stream, or a live accompanist? Set Groove to Off and tap the beat by hand — four taps in a row sets the tempo.
  • Be aware this path will drift over a few minutes, because the app cannot hear that audio. Tap another four-count to re-sync whenever it slips.
  • Loading the actual file is the better option whenever you have it.

Note Board — Quick Tour

Get Going

  • Pick an Ensemble, a Note Set and a Groove, then press Play.
  • Each turn the column pulses top‑to‑bottom (Get Ready, 4‑3‑2‑1), students say the note name, then optionally play it.
  • Click a colored column header to pin a ★ Focus Note — it gets drilled every other turn.

Use Your Own Music

  • Click ♫ Your Own Music next to Groove and load an audio file. The app finds its tempo and plays it locked to the board — no tapping, no drift.
  • That panel opens with its own short guide, and keeps a ⓘ How This Works button for corrections, waltzes, and saving songs.

Good To Know

  • The Instruments list is always shown — leave everyone checked, or uncheck instruments you don't need to keep the board uncluttered. A row shows two octaves when its members read in different registers.
  • Advanced Options holds play order, note‑name reveal, reference pitch, speed trainer, and concert‑pitch display.
  • Save Current stores a class setup by name, and Present goes fullscreen for the projector.

Setups:

Note Selection

How notes are chosen. Random deals evenly from a shuffled bag (no immediate repeats). In order steps low-to-high through the set — good for introducing a new group of notes.

Helpers

Shows each line's own letter name (transposed per instrument) — but only on the last two beats of Say It, so students get a chance to figure it out first.

Sounds the actual concert pitch of each note as it appears, so students can match or tune to it. Best for small groups or personal practice.

Challenge & Display

Nudges the tempo up 2 bpm every turn for a build-up challenge. Works with the built-in grooves — it switches off automatically while a loaded song is running, since that has to hold one steady tempo.

Shows every line at concert pitch instead of each instrument's transposed reading — handy for talking about concert pitch across the whole room.

Click a colored header to pin a focus note (★) — it gets drilled every other turn while the rest of the set fills the gaps. A row shows both octaves when its members read in different registers (e.g. trombone + tuba), and notes past an instrument's range are hidden automatically.
